# Artwork and export notes

The character art was regenerated with the built-in image generation tool, using the approved owl, deer, children, couple and invitation references.

Prompt specification: preserve each identity and outfit; neutral-grey irregular broken pen lines; white opaque figure interiors and transparent surroundings; sparse readable features; no scene, ground, guides or cast shadows; consistent body scale and registration. Keep sixteen core poses per subject, with the listed owl flight/landing, deer forward-facing walk-away, Nayan lean/push-off, child peek and couple-wave additions. The current fringe, half-sleeved wedding dress, polo and tailored shorts remain the clothing references.

The aligned 4× masters, six-cell sheet layout, quarter-size PNGs, transparent tree cutouts, clips and isolated previews are mechanical asset preparation. Tree lines and door leaves were extracted from the approved invitation artwork, not redrawn.

No private source photographs or whole-scene prototype are included.
